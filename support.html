Help & support

A little help for your social calendar. Start with the answers below, or contact the developer if something is not working.

Add and edit a plan

Tap an empty day, type a name and tap the tick. Add @7pm or @1900 at the end of the name to set a time. Tap an existing event to change its details. Touch and hold a day to open its event list and add another plan.

Use Multiple days for a trip or a plan that spans dates. For repeating events, edits currently apply to the whole series; the editor explains this before you change it. Notes and website links live in Notes & links inside the event.

Find a location

Open an event and tap Add location. Type a place, venue or address, then select a suggestion to save its map and address. Previously used locations appear when the search is empty.

If Apple Maps is unavailable, use the option to save the name without a map. Internet access is needed for new suggestions and map content.

See your year and colour your plans

Swipe between months. Pinch inward to see the year, then tap a month to open it. In Settings → Keyword Colours, add a word such as Coffee and choose a colour. Matching event titles pick it up automatically unless you chose a colour explicitly.

Stats counts events matching your keyword rules. A multi-day event counts once; repeating occurrences count separately. You can replay the welcome tutorial from Settings.

Missing iPhone calendar events

Open Settings → iPhone Calendars, enable the connection and select the calendars you want to show. If access was denied, allow calendar access for Socialite in iPhone Settings. Check that the event is visible in Apple Calendar first, then use Refresh in Socialite’s calendar settings.

Socialite reads these events; editing and deleting them happens in Apple Calendar or their original app. A Google calendar can appear through your iPhone’s calendar accounts; Socialite does not have a separate Google sign-in.

Weather is missing

Turn on Show Weather in Settings and allow location access while using the app. Check the update time and any message in the Weather section. Forecasts cover today and up to 15 days ahead, not every day in the year.

New weather needs an internet connection and an available location. Cached forecasts may remain visible while offline. Forecasts are supplied by Open-Meteo and can change.

Copy your free days

Tap the calendar bubble to copy free days. Touch and hold it to choose the period, including weekends-only options. Paste the text into Messages or another app yourself.

Availability depends on local events and the iPhone calendars you have selected. Check that those calendars are enabled and up to date before relying on the result. Socialite will report calendar or storage errors rather than assume missing events mean you are free.

A change did not save

If Socialite displays a saving error, keep the app open and use Retry. Check available device storage. Do not delete or reinstall the app to troubleshoot: local events are stored on the device and could be lost.

Named edits normally save when you finish editing or when the app becomes inactive. An unnamed new event is treated as an unfinished draft. There is not yet a dedicated export or restore feature.

Appearance and accessibility

Choose System, Light or Dark in Settings. Month backgrounds use the Socialite palette in both modes. You can place the free-days bubble on the bottom left or bottom right.

The welcome animation respects the device’s Reduce Motion setting. If text, contrast or a control is difficult to use, include your text-size setting and device model when reporting it.

Report a problem

Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, Socialite version from Settings, what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot can help, but remove private event details first. Do not send passwords or your entire calendar database.

For privacy questions or deletion requests relating to a support conversation, use the same contact details below.
