Privacy policy

Your plans belong to you. This policy explains what Socialite stores on your device and what is sent when you use optional connected features.

Your events stay on your device

Socialite stores the events you create, including titles, dates, times, notes, links, colours, repeat rules and selected places, in the app’s local database. Preferences and keyword-colour rules are also stored on your device. Socialite does not currently provide its own cloud account or cloud sync.

Your device backups may include app data, depending on your Apple backup settings. Your iPhone calendars may be synced separately by Apple or your calendar-account provider.

Your iPhone calendars

Connecting calendars is optional. iOS asks for Full Access because that is the permission needed to read calendar events. Socialite reads the calendars you enable so it can display events, calculate statistics and find free days. It does not create, change or delete events in those calendars.

Imported event details are processed on your device. You can disable the connection in Socialite Settings or revoke calendar access in iPhone Settings.

Places and Apple Maps

When you search for a place, your search text is sent to Apple Maps to provide suggestions and location results. Map previews and opening directions also use Apple’s services. Socialite saves a selected place’s name, address and coordinates with your event.

You can enter a location name without selecting a map result. Typing into the search field still requests suggestions from Apple Maps when an internet connection is available. Apple’s processing is governed by its own privacy information.

Apple Maps privacy information

Optional weather

If you enable weather, Socialite asks for location access while using the app. It obtains a location from iOS and sends latitude and longitude rounded to two decimal places to Open-Meteo over HTTPS, together with the requested forecast parameters. The provider also receives ordinary connection information, such as your IP address. Event titles, notes and calendar contents are not included in weather requests.

The last location supplied by iOS, forecast and update time are cached on your device so forecasts can be reused. Turning weather off stops future scheduled refreshes; it does not erase the existing cache. iOS may clear cached files. You can separately revoke location access in iPhone Settings.

Open-Meteo’s published policy says its troubleshooting logs may contain geographic coordinates and are deleted after 90 days. Its privacy terms govern that service.

Open-Meteo terms and privacy

Sharing and external links

Copy free days writes the availability text to the system clipboard only when you ask. Socialite does not send it to anyone. If you paste it into another app, that app’s privacy practices apply; Apple’s clipboard features may also apply according to your settings.

Opening a link from an event or the help pages opens the destination through your device. Those websites and apps have their own privacy practices.

Analytics, advertising and support

The current app does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not send your event database to the developer. Apple may process diagnostics according to your device and developer-sharing settings.

If you contact support, the developer receives the email address and information you choose to send and uses it to respond and resolve your request. Avoid sending private calendar contents, passwords or sensitive information. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete local events inside Socialite. Clear All Events removes local events, including their notes and saved places, but does not erase keyword rules, preferences, the weather cache or events in your iPhone calendars. Deleting the app removes its local app data; retained device backups are managed through Apple’s settings.

No Socialite account is required, so there is no Socialite account to delete. For personal information you send to support, contact the developer to request access, correction or deletion. Applicable rights depend on your location. You can also raise a concern with your local data-protection authority.

These help pages

These pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising cookies or embedded third-party fonts. The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection and security information to deliver the website, as described in its privacy statement. No Socialite or GitHub account is needed to read the published pages.
