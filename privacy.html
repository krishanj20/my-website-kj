Annum - Social Calendar / privacy

Privacy policy

Annum – Social Calendar is developed by Krishan Jethwa. This policy explains the information used by the app, its Home Screen widgets, this website and support. Most calendar information stays on your device; optional Apple services receive the information needed to provide their features.

Who is responsible

Krishan Jethwa is responsible for Annum and for personal information received through support and beta feedback. You can contact him at krishanjethwa@outlook.com. Apple and the providers of your calendar accounts operate their own services under their own privacy terms.

Annum does not require an account and has no developer-operated server for your calendar. The developer cannot browse or retrieve the events stored on your device. There are no advertisements, data sales or cross-app tracking.

Your events stay on your device

Annum stores the events you create, including titles, dates, times, notes, links, colours, repeat rules and selected places, in the app’s local database. Preferences and keyword-colour rules are also stored on your device. Annum does not currently provide its own cloud account or cloud sync.

Your device backups may include app data, depending on your Apple backup settings. Your iPhone calendars may be synced separately by Apple or your calendar-account provider.

Your iPhone calendars

Connecting calendars is optional. iOS asks for Full Access because that is the permission needed to read calendar events. Annum reads the calendars you enable so it can display events, calculate statistics and find free days. It does not create, change or delete events in those calendars.

Imported event details are processed on your device. You can disable the connection in Annum Settings or revoke calendar access in iPhone Settings.

Home Screen widgets

Annum writes an on-device snapshot of upcoming dates, event titles and times to a shared container used only by Annum and its widget extension. The snapshot covers the next 21 days and includes events from the iPhone calendars you selected. It does not include event notes, saved addresses or location coordinates. The widget reads this snapshot rather than opening the event database.

Widgets display event text on your Home Screen and may be visible to anyone who can see that screen. You choose whether to add or remove them. Widget event text is marked as privacy sensitive for system presentations that support hiding it. Annum does not upload the snapshot to the developer.

Places and Apple Maps

When you search for a place, your search text is sent to Apple Maps to provide suggestions and location results. Map previews and opening directions also use Apple’s services. Annum saves a selected place’s name, address and coordinates with your event.

You can enter a location name without selecting a map result. Typing into the search field still requests suggestions from Apple Maps when an internet connection is available. Apple’s processing is governed by its own privacy information.

Apple Maps privacy information

Optional weather

If you enable weather, Annum asks for location access while using the app. It obtains a location from iOS and requests forecasts from Apple Weather through WeatherKit. Apple receives the location needed to provide the forecast. Event titles, notes and calendar contents are not included in weather requests.

The last location supplied by iOS, forecast and update time are cached on your device so forecasts can be reused. Turning weather off stops future scheduled refreshes; it does not erase the existing cache. iOS may clear cached files. You can separately revoke location access in iPhone Settings.

Apple’s privacy information governs its weather service. The Apple Weather mark and a link to the weather data sources are available in Annum Settings → Weather.

Sharing and external links

Copy free days writes the availability text to the system clipboard only when you ask. Annum does not send it to anyone. If you paste it into another app, that app’s privacy practices apply; Apple’s clipboard features may also apply according to your settings.

Opening a link from an event or the help pages opens the destination through your device. Those websites and apps have their own privacy practices.

Diagnostics, TestFlight and support

Annum has no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. It does not upload your event database to the developer. Apple may provide app usage and crash information under its App Store, device diagnostic and developer-sharing settings. Diagnostic information can include device model, operating-system version, app version and technical crash details.

If you use a TestFlight beta, Apple automatically collects testing usage and crash information and shares it with the developer; this collection cannot be switched off while testing through TestFlight. The information can include your device model, device identifier, app and iOS versions, sessions and crashes, and your name and email address for email invitations. Feedback and screenshots you submit are also shared. The developer uses this information to investigate problems and improve Annum, keeps it only while needed for that work, and does not share TestFlight information with third parties. Avoid including private event details in screenshots or reports. Apple’s TestFlight privacy information explains its own retention and processing.

If you email support, your email address and the information you choose to include are received through the developer’s email provider, Microsoft Outlook. They are used to answer your request, fix problems and keep a record of the resolution. Support correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the request, handle follow-up questions or meet legal obligations, and is then deleted. Do not send passwords or your calendar database.

Your choices and deletion

Local events are retained until you delete them or remove the app. You can edit or delete individual events. Clear All Events removes Annum’s local events, notes and saved places; it does not remove preferences, keyword rules, the weather cache or events in your iPhone calendars. Imported calendar data follows your calendar connection and selected calendars.

The widget snapshot is replaced when its calendar content changes while Annum is running. After changing events or calendar selections, open Annum to update the widgets. Removing a widget hides it but does not itself delete the shared snapshot. The weather cache is replaced after successful refreshes and may be cleared by iOS; switching weather off stops scheduled refreshes but does not erase the last cache.

Deleting Annum removes its local app data. Shared widget files can remain while an associated extension is still installed. iCloud or computer backups, external calendars and information already shared with another app are managed separately through Apple, your calendar provider or that other app. Offloading the app preserves its documents and data.

You can turn off calendar integration and weather in Annum Settings, or revoke Calendar and Location permissions in iPhone Settings. These features are optional; without permission their connected information will not be available. No Annum account exists to delete.

Why information is used and your rights

Local event and calendar processing provides the planning features you request. Optional calendar and location access requires your permission, which you can withdraw in iPhone Settings. Apple separately determines the legal basis for its services.

Where UK or European data-protection law applies to information received by the developer, support and technical feedback are handled on the basis of legitimate interests in answering requests and improving the reliability of Annum. Information is used to comply with a legal obligation where one applies. Providing support information is voluntary, although a reply may not be possible without contact details and enough information about the problem.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information held by the developer. You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email the contact above to exercise these rights. The developer may need proportionate information to confirm your identity and cannot retrieve data that exists only on your device.

You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office or your local data-protection authority. Annum does not use your information for decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Service providers, international processing and security

Optional online features contact Apple for weather, maps and related content. Support emails use Microsoft Outlook, and the public website uses GitHub Pages. These providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy notices describe processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards; the developer does not operate a separate international calendar database.

Annum relies on iOS application isolation, device data protection and Apple’s secure service connections. The widget snapshot uses iOS file protection. Device passcodes, backup choices and access to your screen also affect the protection of your information. No service or device can guarantee absolute security.
