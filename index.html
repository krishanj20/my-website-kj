July 2024 — Present · London

Finding structure
in the markets.

I research systematic trading strategies and build the models and tools that support them. The work connects quantitative research with the day-to-day demands of live trading.

work in practice.

03 / Where the curiosity began

June — August 2020 · Göttingen, Germany

Before market models,
particle physics.

Particle Physics ResearchUniversity of Göttingen

I worked on top-quark pair production research in a CERN collaboration, using C++, multivariate analysis and Monte Carlo simulation.

It was an early chance to work with a question that could not be answered by intuition alone: build a model, test it against data, and keep asking what the evidence actually says.
