Saturday 3 October · Seville

Your Seville day

Plaza de España, Santa Cruz, flamenco at 5 pm, and a great traditional dinner.

ItineraryTickets & maps
Assumption: this starts at Hotel Giralda Center. Aim to arrive at Casa del Flamenco by 4:30 pm for your 5 pm show.
09:30

Breakfast near the hotel

Keep it relaxed: today has plenty of walking and the dinner will be late.

10:15–11:15

Plaza de España

Walk the canal, find your favourite tiled province alcove and take photos before the centre gets busier.

Walk from hotel
11:15–12:15

Parque de María Luisa → Jardines de Murillo

Take the shady, slower route north towards the historic centre. You will arrive at the edge of Santa Cruz.

Start the walk
12:30–13:30

Early tapas lunch in Santa Cruz

Keep lunch light: two or three tapas each is enough. Then enjoy the lanes around Plaza de Doña Elvira and Calle Agua.

Walk into Santa Cruz
13:45–14:45

Casa de Pilatos

Your Alcázar alternative: a compact, beautiful palace of tiled patios and courtyard gardens. Allow an hour, then take a drink break.

Walk to Casa de Pilatos
15:45–16:25

Coffee, then make your way to flamenco

Head back through Santa Cruz without rushing. This buffer is deliberate—its streets are a maze.

Walk to Casa del Flamenco
17:00–18:00

Flamenco at Casa del Flamenco

Enjoy the show. Your venue is on Calle Ximénez de Enciso, in the heart of Santa Cruz.

18:15–20:15

Giralda exterior → Cathedral → Torre del Oro

Walk through Plaza del Triunfo and down to the Guadalquivir for the evening light. Stop for a drink before dinner if you fancy it.

Walk to the river via the Cathedral
20:30–21:00 · Dinner

Two brilliant traditional options

Both are worth your evening. Choose Casa Morales for an old-bodega tapas dinner near the Cathedral; choose El Rinconcillo for the historic experience if the walk-in bar queue is reasonable.

Casa MoralesTraditional Seville/Andalusia · carrillada, spinach & chickpeas, bacalao pavía · around 15 min from the river El RinconcilloHistoric tapas bar since 1670 · walk-in downstairs bar only if the queue looks sensible · around 25 min from the river
