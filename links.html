Dinner: your two options

Best no-fuss choice

Casa Morales

An 1850 bodega near the Cathedral, with classic Sevillian and Andalusian tapas. Good choices: carrillada ibérica, espinacas con garbanzos and bacalao pavía.

Historic experience

El Rinconcillo

The city’s oldest bar (since 1670). The upstairs restaurant is booked; the downstairs tapas bar is walk-in and standing-table focused. Try it only if the queue looks manageable.
